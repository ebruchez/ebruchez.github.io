Background

I grew up reading comic books (known as “bandes dessinées” in French). My taste leans towards Franco-Belgian comics, but I am also an admirer of American comics like Mickey Mouse and Donald Duck, as well as some American comic strips including Peanuts and Calvin and Hobbes. I am not into superhero comics or manga, but that might still happen, who knows?

Les Fils d'El Topo - Tome 1: Caïn

★★★★★ Bédéthèque
Oct 1, 2026 Story: Alexandro Jodorowsky Art: José Ladrönn 2016 Reread
  • I reread this after a few years.
  • Yes, this is Jodorowsky and his crazy ideas, in the spirit of El Topo, his movie. You like it or you don’t like it.
  • But you have to admit that the art is amazing, from beginning to end.
  • There is a lot of magic in this story, which otherwise is set in a Wild West environment.
  • For example, the “saint”’s tomb, with its golden pillars, and magic bridge which judges the visitor.
  • The caricature of religion echoes scenes of El Topo.
  • As always, there are disturbing scenes: the cripples, rape, murder.
  • But there is also beauty: Abel and his mother, for example.
  • I found the scene with the group of blind very human. The huge lady, offering her body, but in joy and fun. Where else would you see such a scene?
  • Finally, the young virgin Nina, following Cain as if preordained, is troubling and intriguing.

Lefranc - 36. La Régate

★★★★★ Bédéthèque
Sep 29, 2026 Story: Roger Seiter Art: Régric 2025 French
  • Comments on Bédéthèque are not positive: characters’ faces not nice, title not appropriate, story boring, etc.
  • I didn’t find this album to be that bad.
  • I do find the location of the action interesting.
  • The bombing of the ship in the end is indeed surprising from Lefranc.
  • The title doesn’t bother me at all. It does start with a boat race, and then unplanned events happen. The ocean is present throughout.
  • I am not sure I buy the character of Thea, who is oblivious to her father’s shady dealings.

Les mondes de Thorgal - La jeunesse, Tome 10. Sydonia

★★★★★ Bédéthèque
Sep 2026 Story: Yann Art: Roman Surzhenko 2022 French

Not a bad story. Harald’s young daughter shows immense cruelty, but also cunning. She convinces the powerful of the city to help her seize power. Thorgal helps Sveynn reconquer the town. We learn that Sveynn almost killed Thorgal, who is now between life and death. Sveynn hopes Thorgal survives and pardons him.

One particularity of this series is that the hero is a woman and women constitute the majority of the important characters of the stories (even though the author, Roger Leloup, is a man). This never bothered me the least as a boy and, as an adult, I still love the series.

Achille Talon

Bédéthèque
By: Greg

This series has been a favorite of mine ever since I encountered it (only the original albums by Greg). It is probably impossible to translate this to English or other languages, as the humor is often tied to the French language. Greg even went to the US for a while to promote his character, without success.

Alix

Wikipedia
By: Jacques Martin

Jacques Martin created this genre where the story takes place during Roman antiquity. He started with Alix, and later created a modern counterpart with Lefranc.

Lefranc

Bédéthèque
By: Jacques Martin

The other major character created by Jacques Martin; some of the stories in this series are just very good. My favorite might be Le Repaire du loup (Bédéthèque), drawn by Bob de Moor, which is a masterpiece of the genre. Unfortunately, de Moor was not allowed by the Studios Hergé to continue working on Lefranc.

Blake et Mortimer (the original series)

Bédéthèque Wikipedia
By: Edgar P. Jacobs

You can just read and re-read these albums without ever tiring of them. See also my 2004 review of La Damnation d’Edgar P. Jacobs, the biography of Jacobs by Benoît Mouchart and François Rivière.

Thorgal

Bédéthèque
Story: Jean Van Hamme Art: Grzegorz Rosiński

An excellent series mixing northern mythology and science-fiction at times, always with adventure. The art by Grzegorz Rosiński evolved over time from album 1 to album 36 (1980 to 2018); album 37, from 2019, features a new cartoonist, Fred Vignaux. Writers: Jean Van Hamme (1–29), Yves Sente (30–34), Xavier Dorison (35), Yann (36–37).
