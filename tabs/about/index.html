About

My name is Erik Bruchez. I was born and raised in Switzerland, where I spent the first 25 years of my life. Since 1997 I have lived in the San Francisco Bay area (with a hiatus around 2005), now in Foster City. I am married with two kids. I work as a programmer (and homme à tout faire) for a small company, Orbeon, that I co-founded back in 1999 with my friend Alex. I love technology, music, and philosophy. I believe that we have to keep refining our thoughts and view of the world to make any progress in life.

Outside of work, I restore and collect vintage typewriters and vintage computers, and I maintain Mascagni.org, an online archive dedicated to the Italian opera composer Pietro Mascagni (1863–1945) that I originally created in the late 1990s and recently revived.
