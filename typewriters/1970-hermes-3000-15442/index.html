Notes & Description

I bought this Hermes 3000 at an estate sale, after going 3 times, and buying it on the 50% off day. Even so, the machine wasn’t exactly cheap, but it is in really good condition, with only a tiny crack in one of the plastic parts on the carriage.

On the other hand the case top had some rust on some of the handle hardware, which I intend to fix.

This is the “HP-2/66” of “boxy” version of the Hermes 3000. It has mostly metal parts, except for a few plastic parts on the carriage. The following year’s model (“HP-2/71”) introduced an all-plastic body.

Like many, I do prefer the look of the “curvy” 3000 with its green keys. But I still think that this model is quite beautiful as well - and it types really well!

The typeface is Pica (“S 6”).
