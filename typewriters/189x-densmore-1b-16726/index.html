Notes & Description

Most of the parts of this Densmore 1b appear present. I note a missing part for the ribbon guide, and a missing part at the center of the carriage.

I cannot find a serial in the usual places, and I am told that it can be missing on these machines.

The paint is almost entirely bust, and so is the plating. This machine will need a full restoration.

Densmore 1B1:

  • 38 keys

  • plain bearings (no ball bearings)

  • rubber ring platen knobs

  • instructions decal on the paper table

  • Densmore in script on the front of the basket

I have replacement decals. The bad news is that the paint and plating are a lot of work, but the good news, compared with other machines, is that there are no pinstripes to redo!

UPDATE: I found the serial 6711 on the left side after brushing off dust and rust.
