Marcus Miller in Cully

Apr 9, 2005 by Erik Bruchez
2 min
Music

I just attended on Thursday Marcus Miller’s concert in Cully, Switzerland. This must have been my fourth of fifth Marcus Miller concert: I have seen him in Montreux, Switzerland at least once, and at least a couple of times in California, in particular a double concert at Kimballs East. I have always returned enthusiastic.

This time we were the first in line to get in, and so we managed to be right in the center in front of the stage, a great spot if you want to become deaf, but also of course being about 30 centimeters from the musicians, it’s quite an experience. My brother was wearing a Miles Davis t-shirt, which attracted Stewart’s attention during a solo. Oh, and beware, you might get Dean Brown’s guitar in your face if you’re not careful.

We were told that the band was stuck for seven hours on their way from Paris, and they didn’t have time to do a sound check. So the concert kind of started sound check style with the tune Bruce Lee, Poogie Bell on the drums, progressively joined by the other musicians. Finally, Marcus arrived on the stage, dancing to the music, looking cool as usual! It may sound cliché to say this, but he has music in his blood.

The band consisted of the usual Poogie Bell on drums, Michael “Patches” Stewart on trumpet, Dean Brown on guitar, with Keith Anderson on sax, and Bobby Sparks on keyboards. Another guy also played the keyboard and a second bass from time to time. For the second encore, the band was joined by Nino G, the “human beatbox” as they say. Nino played with the band of Reto Webber just before Marcus, and he was a real kick!

You have to admire those touring musicians. This is how music should be done: put together an album, and then go on tour. I can only imagine that this must be at the same time fun and exhausting for musicians: if you look at Marcus’s tour dates, you will see that he is touring from February 26 to June 5 for a total of 43 concerts. From April 6 to May 7, that’s 26 concerts over 32 days in Europe!

I look forward to seeing him on May 14 in Fairfield, California.
